The Product Development Minefield

We specialise in the disciplines of New Product Design. Many early-stage consumer brands inadvertently design simple mechanical goods that look exceptional on screen but are incredibly expensive or physically impossible to injection-mould. Correcting a flawed tool mid-production can lead to costs spiralling into the thousands and delay your retail launch by months.

We bridge the gap between creative product design and factory-ready manufacturing. We help UK startups and established brands de-risk their engineering, lower their component counts, and protect their investment capital before committing to mass production.

Our Phase-Gated Service Menu

Phase 1: Feasibility Study & DFM Alignment Review (Gate 01)

Comprehensive evaluation of your existing CAD files, aesthetics, and concept architecture. We catch injection-moulding flaws early.

Phase 2: Detailed Engineering Design & Component Optimisation (Gate 02)

Refining assembly architecture, wall thicknesses, draft angles, and wall interfaces while strictly reducing overall part count.

Phase 3: Prototyping & Functional Verification (Gate 03)

Sourcing and testing high-fidelity physical representations to test fit, form, mechanical actuation, and tactile response.

Phase 4: Tooling Handover & Production Localisation (Gate 04)

Compiling final golden production CAD formats, navigating manufacturer procurement, and overseeing tool sign-off.
